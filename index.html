How it works

Less scanning. More signal.

FTJA turns your own background and judgment into a local, reviewable search loop. It does not decide your career for you; it clears the noise so your attention goes to the roles that deserve it.

◌
01 / GROUND

Start with your story

Bring a resume, portfolio, or other files. FTJA distills them into a profile your judge can actually use.

⌁
02 / SHAPE

Make your standard explicit

Confirm suggested roles and keywords, then define the signals, preferences, and dealbreakers behind a real yes.

↘
03 / FILTER

Spend judgment where it matters

Public LinkedIn postings move through a fast keyword filter, a lightweight sentence check, and a deeper profile match.

↻
04 / LEARN

Keep the loop yours

Record apply or skip and why. FTJA proposes a rubric change for your review instead of silently rewriting your standard.
